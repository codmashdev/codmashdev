- Layout, animation templates and the `team7-dance.gif`, `rasengan-run.gif`, and `chibi-walk.gif` assets are adapted/reused from the user-provided [Megha Mittal profile reference](https://github.com/Meghamittal0920/Meghamittal0920). Original creator: [Megha Mittal](https://github.com/Meghamittal0920). No ownership of the original reference or anime characters is claimed.
- Male Codmash artwork was generated for this profile with the built-in imagegen tool; source PNGs and optimized WebP exports are included.
- Technology marks: [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/), distributed under CC0; brands retain their trademarks. HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, React and Git icon paths were included in the reference; the other icons were retrieved from Simple Icons.
- Embedded typefaces: [Space Grotesk](https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk) and [JetBrains Mono](https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono). SIL Open Font License texts are included in `assets/fonts/`.
- Codmash portfolio links and service descriptions are based on [codmash.com](https://codmash.com/). Account statistics and contribution dates are fetched from GitHub's public `codmashdev` profile.
